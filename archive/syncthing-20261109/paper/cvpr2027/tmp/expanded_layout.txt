 621   Ethics and data. The real-image analyses use existing
 622   aggregate outputs from ocular research datasets. No par-
 623   ticipant images, identiﬁers or newly reconstructed human
 624   anatomy are included in this draft. Re-identiﬁcation-related
 625   measurements can expose sensitive information even when
 626   analysis uses accessible data. Dataset permissions, the basis
 627   for secondary analysis and institutional review requirements
 628   must be conﬁrmed before submission; an ethics approval
 629   or exemption is not asserted here. Template and reference
 630   construction should also exclude related people from target
 631   pools where family information is relevant.

 632   8. Conclusion
 633   Identity Ladder asks what evidence supports interpreting
 634   image similarity as preservation of an individual. Synthetic
 635   controls distinguish attenuation from incorrect detail, while
 636   saved fundus and iris comparisons show how photometry,
 637   representation and correspondence change the meaning of
 638   a high score. The protocol separates these questions and
 639   speciﬁes a spatial atlas and inpainting extension. Complet-
 640   ing that empirical benchmark, validating a combined iden-
 641   tity measure and evaluating modern reconstruction outputs
 642   remain open work.
