# Evidence coverage for population audits of conditional generation

Existing experiments measure donor/reference and metric behavior in human-anatomy datasets. Model-output and population-stratified generator audits below are proposed extensions, not executed results.

| ID | Question | Evidence coverage | Missing validation |
|---|---|---|---|
| P01 | What is conditioned on, and which identity unit should be preserved? | Task/reference definition developed | A model-specific conditioning contract and an explicit treatment of unidentifiable detail. Human anatomical relationships need domain-specific replacements outside the current data. |
| P02 | Which target strata, donor cohorts and acquisition domains are being compared? | Donor matching available; generator strata not measured | Prespecified target groups, metadata availability, independent-unit counts and matched task-difficulty strata. A modality or dataset contrast is not a demographic disparity. |
| P03 | Is the distance implementation and support contract explicit? | Substantial saved implementation-probe coverage | Deployment parity and task-specific mask-boundary/three-dimensional conventions. A metric name alone does not specify an evaluator. |
| P04 | Does the score notice attenuation toward a global or matched population mean? | Mechanism evidence; MRI provisional | Actual generated completions, information recoverable from their context and a distinction between legitimate mean prediction and harmful loss of relevant variation. |
| P05 | Can omitted detail be distinguished from plausible but incorrect detail? | Controlled proxy evidence | Paired model/control comparisons at the same spatial scope, independent anatomical error assessments and a defined cost of omission versus incorrect structure. |
| P06 | Is acquisition variation separated from identity or anatomy? | Partial; MRI gate unresolved | Accepted correspondence/QC where needed and task-specific checks that nuisance invariance retains meaningful anatomy. |
| P07 | Are anatomical structure, downstream utility and spatial support tested? | Synthetic/partial spatial evidence | Independent real-image structure/usefulness criteria, irregular masks and completed object-relative scale studies. Downstream agreement alone is not identity validation. |
| P08 | How does a generator compare with the ladder within each target stratum? | Proposed readout; no subgroup generator experiment | Frozen model outputs, matched eligible targets/support, sampling budgets and paired output-minus-control profiles for each metric, rung and target group. |
| P09 | Do apparent group differences survive a common reference and comparable task difficulty? | Proposed comparison; not measured | Comparable mask/location/acquisition conditions, uncertainty in reference pools and group differences, and sensitivity to group-specific reference variance. Descriptive gaps are not causal fairness findings. |
| P10 | Are between-target variation and within-context sampling variation separated? | Conceptual controls only | Repeated outputs at a fixed seed/sample budget, conditional diversity and target-specific fidelity measurements. Reduced variance or mean preference alone does not establish mode collapse. |
| P11 | Do profiles support stable, independently meaningful evaluation decisions? | Metric dependence partial; model/group decisions unvalidated | Independent quality criteria, target-cluster uncertainty with repeated masks/seeds, eligibility/missingness and worst-group summaries where estimable, and frozen aggregation choices. |
| P12 | Which parts transfer beyond inpainting to other generative settings? | Proposed extension; human-anatomy evidence only | New domain-specific controls and validation. Unconditional generation has no intended target individual; paired identity fidelity cannot be applied without that condition and requires separate distribution/coverage criteria. |

The complete scope and sources are defined in population_audit.json. POPULATION_AUDIT_FRAMING.md distinguishes target strata, donor references and acquisition domains. The earlier BraTS decision map is retained as an application-specific history, not the current scope.
