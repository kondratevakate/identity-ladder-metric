## Evidence boundary

Article type: a cross-anatomy evaluation audit, not a new restoration network. Domains: brain MRI, fundus photographs, faces and iris. Comparators: repeated capture, bilateral structures, related donors where available, context donors, independent means and negative controls. Brain and fundus remain provisional. The full iris P3 extension is absent. No trained-model inpainting leaderboard or clinical validation is claimed.

The abstract's brain coordinates 0.413 and 0.717 come from the random-region LR MSE R_repeat profile in donor_ladder_brain_metrics.json. These are different anatomical and measurement scopes; the abstract explicitly retains that distinction.

## Three title candidates

1. **Identity Ladder: Auditing Image Similarity for Individual Anatomy** — recommended and applied. It names the project, the evaluated object and the intended interpretation without claiming a solved identity metric.
2. **When Similar Images Lose Individual Detail: A Cross-Anatomy Donor-Ladder Audit** — highlights the failure question, but places the standard search phrase image similarity less directly.
3. **Image Similarity, Bilateral Anatomy, and Individual Detail: A Donor-Ladder Audit** — exposes bilateral anatomy, at the cost of a longer list-like title and a weaker project identifier.

Rejected framings include a universal identity metric, anatomy-preserving reconstruction, a state-of-the-art benchmark result, and a completed clinical validation because the current sources do not support them.
