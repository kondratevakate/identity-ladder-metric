## MRI: structural registration and harmonization require different choices

**SPEAR — MICCAI 2026, Experimental Settings.** [Paper](https://papers.miccai.org/miccai-2026/paper/4287_paper.pdf). Inter-subject **T1-weighted brain MRI**, IXI/OASIS/IBSR18; images are affinely pre-aligned and intensity-normalized before deformable registration. Subject-disjoint training/validation/test split is 7:1:2; moving/fixed pairs are generated inside each split. Test labels are reserved for Dice evaluation; deformation folding is reported separately as the proportion with nonpositive Jacobian determinant. Training uses an auxiliary segmenter, so its labels are not an independent test endpoint. The stated compute is an L40S with 48 GB. The [linked repository](https://github.com/ZedKing12138/SPEAR-registration), inspected at `92508d178ed0f2b6e0e34ea918ea0fe34b18ce90`, has a minimal framework README; executable pretrained reproduction was not established.

**M2CG — MICCAI 2026, sections 3–4.** [Paper](https://papers.miccai.org/miccai-2026/paper/5386_paper.pdf). Separate inter-patient brain and intra-patient cardiac tasks, eight datasets. Brain OASIS is split 314/20/80 and further datasets test generalization. Reports segmentation DSC and negative-Jacobian percentage separately. Preprocessing details are partly delegated to an older reference; the venue page lists no code. It supports the evaluation structure, not an immediately runnable published pipeline on QTIM.

**Anatomy- and Site-Guided 3D Patch Diffusion — MICCAI 2026, section 3.** [Paper](https://papers.miccai.org/miccai-2026/paper/3157_paper.pdf). T1w harmonization preprocessing: HD-BET skull stripping, ANTs bias-field correction, FSL affine alignment to 1 mm MNI, crop 160 × 192 × 160, median intensity normalization. Traveling-subject SRPBS evaluation contains 97 scans of nine people at 11 sites. Histogram Wasserstein distance is distinguished from paired-subject SSIM/PCC and downstream anatomy/clinical analyses. [Official README](https://github.com/barna-hache/PatchLevelDiffusionMRIHarmonization), commit `6d1d21e9c52087c87dc5e32cd2b618f9a6d2dc5b`, confirms the preprocessing sequence and links weights, but still calls the work a MIDL 2026 submission under a different title. Resolve this paper/release-version mismatch before exact reproduction. A learned target-site embedding requires a target seen during training; unseen source-site claims do not imply arbitrary unseen target sites.

**Implication for our QC (study-specific inference).** Record MRI contrast and native voxel geometry before choosing a model. A pipeline validated for pre-aligned T1w inter-subject MRI cannot be declared suitable for all contrasts, repeat scans, or contralateral regions. Do not copy deformable registration from a registration benchmark into harmonization by default: the harmonization paper uses affine normalization. Preserve individual anatomy and pathology rather than optimizing it away. Keep anatomical overlap/landmark endpoints separate from geometric invertibility and scanner-effect endpoints. None of these papers establishes our existing 90% release threshold or supplies transferable numerical acceptance cutoffs.

## Independent anatomy checks

**Uncovering Interpretable Point-Wise Image Correspondences — MICCAI 2026, section 3.** [Paper](https://papers.miccai.org/miccai-2026/paper/5323_paper.pdf). Brain experiment: 32 annotated fiducials for 30 OASIS subjects and MNI, after FreeSurfer affine pre-alignment at 1 mm. Segmentation training/validation images are separate from correspondence-evaluation images. TRE is Euclidean error in millimetres; success detection rate is the fraction within a specified distance. The approach estimates landmarks from segmentation features; the evaluation annotations are independent ground truth. No public code is linked on the venue page.

**Implication (study-specific inference).** Our fitted feature matches, an auxiliary segmentation, and a plausible checkerboard cannot themselves certify registration. Obtain independent local anatomical evidence and report physical units plus coverage. A positive Jacobian is a geometric check, not proof that a sulcus or vessel is correctly matched. The paper does not validate a three-landmark rule for our crops; that remains a local protocol choice needing justification.

## Real MRI denoising: use real acquisition controls

**YADO: Rethinking Real-World MRI Denoising — ECCV 2026, sections 2 and 5; supplementary noise analysis.** [Author page and paper](https://srassmann.github.io/papers/yado/), [official code](https://github.com/Deep-MI/YADO). Physical Noise2Noise uses repeated acquisitions; Renoise2Noise extends training when repeats are unavailable. Evaluation distinguishes physical acquisition noise from synthetic additive-noise removal. Independent noisy references are discussed explicitly; the noise analysis uses a leave-one-out repeated-scan average. Synthetic white Gaussian/Rician degradation is not treated as sufficient evidence of real acquisition-noise removal. The authors also compare modelling objectives with a common backbone.

**Implication (study-specific inference).** Keep real QTIM repeat scans as a distinct control, with acquisition differences and alignment residuals documented; they are not automatically pure independent thermal-noise repeats. Do not call any repeat or its average ground truth without checking these assumptions. Do not add learned denoising to our registration inputs merely because it improves appearance: that could alter precisely the individual details under study. Denoising experiments remain deferred.

## Fundus: structural correspondences and modality-specific tests

**UniVessel: Cross-domain Controllable Generation Enables Zero-shot Fundus Image Analysis — MICCAI 2026, sections 3–4.** [Paper](https://papers.miccai.org/miccai-2026/paper/1262_paper.pdf). Separates vessel/bifurcation labels from synthesized appearance. Registration learning uses warped image pairs with known affine correspondences or anatomically meaningful bifurcations; testing uses FIRE. Segmentation uses LSCI and ultra-widefield photography, so results are task- and modality-specific. The [official repository](https://github.com/kaiwenli325/UniVessel), commit `e6996cc010457032cba8030524ee9d160e640b2e`, says “Coming soon”; runnable training/inference and weights were not established.

**Implication (study-specific inference).** Independent vessel bifurcations are relevant evidence for same-eye fundus alignment. FIRE evaluation does not establish the validity of forcing fellow eyes or different people into pixelwise vessel agreement. Preserve these comparison roles explicitly. A generative structural guide is not neutral preprocessing for an identity-detail experiment. The existing SuperRetina-on-G1 adapter is exploratory, not the official FIRE reproduction and not a literature-validated choice for every GRAPE role.

## Iris: pupil geometry matters, but generated normalization is a confound

**When Humans Judge Irises — WACV 2026 GAPBio workshop, sections 3–4.** [Paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/WACV2026W/GAPBio/papers/Mitcheff_When_Humans_Judge_Irises_Pupil_Size_Normalization_as_an_Aid_WACVW_2026_paper.pdf), [release](https://github.com/CVRL/Human-Iris-Judge). Studies human iris verification with variable pupil sizes, linear rubber-sheet versus learned nonlinear deformation, and authentic versus synthetic pairs. Excludes images used to train the nonlinear deformation model. Direction of deformation affects compression/interpolation; the study also applies gamma correction. These are experimental conditions, not a universal preprocessing recommendation. The study is a workshop paper, not CVPR/ECCV main-conference evidence, and it does not validate Open-IRIS on UPOL/UTIRIS.

**Implication (study-specific inference).** Review pupil/limbus geometry, dilation, eyelids/eyelashes and visible-light versus NIR domain separately. Prefer documented geometric mapping and explicit occlusion evidence before considering a learned texture-changing normalizer. Such a normalizer would confound identity-detail retention. No matched 2026 benchmark/checkpoint covering both current iris modalities was verified here; leave that gap explicit.

## Simulation does not replace real validation

**Validation of simulation-based evaluation by inclusion of unlabeled data and meta-ablation — MICCAI 2026, methods and OCT experiment.** [Paper](https://papers.miccai.org/miccai-2026/paper/5527_paper.pdf). Mixes simulated and real data to reveal a simulation realism gap; demonstration is nonuniform **3D OCT**, not fundus photography or T1w brain MRI. Jointly varying simulation and compensation models helps identify missing effects.

**Implication (study-specific inference).** Known synthetic transforms can verify our coordinate handling, including reflection, but cannot replace anatomical validation on the real frozen cases. This supports the distinction, not direct transplantation of the OCT procedure.

## Release feasibility follow-up after authorized execution

No weights were present in the inspected repository tree and no GitHub release assets were available. Code availability is therefore established; availability of the paper-trained checkpoint is not.
